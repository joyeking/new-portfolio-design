Field note · Feb 7, 2025

Design choices should earn their place

A visual pattern only helps when it makes the content clearer, the task easier, or the character of the work more specific.

Interaction with a purpose

Motion and depth earn their place when they show relationships, reveal a process, or help someone understand a space. Without that job, they compete with the information.

Start with the human task

Tools change quickly; the questions that guide a useful design remain steady. Who is here, what are they trying to do, and what is getting in their way?

Structure before shape

A modular layout can organize unlike pieces of information, but only when each size and position reflects a real difference in importance.

Material should clarify

A surface treatment can suggest what is touchable or important. If it does not make the interface easier to read, it is only another layer.

Typography is part of the route

Type scale, line length, and spacing shape how someone moves through the message. A distinctive headline cannot compensate for body copy that is difficult to read.
