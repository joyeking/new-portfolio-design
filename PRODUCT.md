# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients and collaborators reviewing the designer’s work. This audience is inferred from the portfolio’s existing project pages and contact flow.

## Product Purpose

Present Yohannes Assefa’s digital design, website, identity, and graphic design work so visitors can understand the problem behind a project, the design approach, and how to begin a conversation. The focus on problem and approach is confirmed by the user’s redesign brief.

## Positioning

The portfolio explains how design decisions respond to a specific visitor need instead of presenting finished screens without context. This is the user’s requested emphasis.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static multi-page portfolio generated from `build_site.mjs`.
- Existing project facts, client testimonials, screenshots, contact information, and portfolio routes are source material; do not invent business outcomes or client claims.
- The user asked to remove references to how many projects appear in the portfolio and to replace generic layouts across the project.
- Project-specific scope, schedule, and content figures remain factual case-study details where useful; portfolio-wide project totals and decorative project numbering are removed.

## Evidence on Hand

- Detailed digital project records and an archive of identity, editorial, campaign, and product work in `build_site.mjs`.
- Project images in `assets/work/` and `assets/site/`.
- Client testimonials and contact details in the site generator.

## Product Principles

- Show the human problem before the visual treatment.
- Explain the system of decisions behind each design.
- Use specific, verifiable facts and preserve the source of each claim.
- Let the visual work lead while giving visitors enough context to understand it.
