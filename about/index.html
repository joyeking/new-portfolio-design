The design system

A way of working that follows the problem.

Listen before drawing

Understand the people, the business, and what is making the current experience difficult.

Define the decision

Agree on what the visitor needs to understand or do, and what evidence will show progress.

Organise the content

Build a route through information before choosing how each screen should look.

Refine in context

Review working screens with real copy and check the experience across devices.

Leave a useful system

Deliver the components, type, and guidance needed to keep the work clear as it grows.
